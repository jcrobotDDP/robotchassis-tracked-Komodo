6.1 Product Overview

The KOMODO-01 is a general-purpose tracked robot chassis that combines advanced performance with precision engineering. Its dual-motor differential drive system uses brushless DC motors and planetary reducers, providing powerful and smooth driving force.

The chassis's unique suspension system has been independently developed, offering excellent torsional strength and lateral rigidity, providing outstanding stability and precise suspension tuning optimization.

The KOMODO-01 is equipped with a 48V 30Ah lithium battery as standard (48V 50Ah or custom options available), with a BMS battery management system. The 150mm wide high-wear-resistant tracks provide excellent obstacle crossing ability and environmental adaptability.
